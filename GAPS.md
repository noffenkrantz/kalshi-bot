# Pinnacle vs Kalshi: gap tracker

*Simulated only, nothing is traded. 0 snapshots. Fair price = Pinnacle's odds with its margin removed. Edge = what a contract is worth at that price minus what Kalshi charges for it, after Kalshi's fee. Closing value = Pinnacle's last fair price before the game minus what we paid: positive on average means the gaps were real.*

*No real market* = buy and sell prices more than 10¢ apart (nobody quoting tightly: room to post orders). The gap and edge columns use only the prices with a real market.
